ratio on your phone

Set this up once. After that, on any recipe page tap Share, pick ratio, and the recipe opens as a ratio.

iPhone: make a Shortcut (about a minute)

  1. Open the Shortcuts app and tap +.
  2. Tap the name at the top, rename it ratio, then tap Details and turn on Show in Share Sheet. Tap Done.
  3. A box appears: “Receive Any input from Share Sheet”. Tap Any and leave only URLs and Safari web pages ticked.
  4. Tap Search Actions, find Open URLs and add it.
  5. Tap its URL box, paste the address below, then tap Shortcut Input above the keyboard so it sits right after url=.
    https://ratio.clocknumbers.com/share?url=
    Copied
  6. Tap Done. Now in Safari, on a recipe, tap Share and choose ratio. The first time, iPhone asks to allow it to open the page: tap Always Allow.

To also get ratio on your Home Screen: open ratio.clocknumbers.com in Safari, tap Share (under ⋯ on iOS 26 and later), then Add to Home Screen.

Android: install the app (30 seconds)

  1. Open ratio.clocknumbers.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen or Install app, then Install.
  3. On any recipe, in Chrome or another app, tap Share and choose ratio. It may be under More the first time.
